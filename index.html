    EDITORIAL SYSTEM

    Aura Originality Engine

    Fingerprint-first

    Every imported story keeps its own editorial architecture. Aura checks the essentials — useful depth, SEO, images, ads and link opportunities — without forcing every topic into one repeated article skeleton.

    Reader situation→Search intent→Unique architecture→Visual story→SEO + monetization

    The new prompt uses a cross-batch editorial-history manifest and similarity rejection tests so openings, headings, examples, action devices, closings and photography do not quietly drift back into templates.

    Every image also gets its own art direction from the section it sits in: shot, lens, light, time of day, setting, people and props, chosen so no two images in a post (or in your recent posts) repeat.

    Ad placement

    Ads go only where they fit the text around them, are always labelled, and use sponsored/nofollow links. Longer posts get more placements; short ones stay light.

    Side cards on themes that remove styles

    If your WordPress user is an Administrator, side cards float beside the text automatically. Otherwise WordPress removes the layout code and they show as neat inline cards. To float them anyway, paste this into WordPress → Appearance → Customize → Additional CSS once.

    Affiliate & ad library

    Keywords decide where each link fits. The IDs go into the ChatGPT prompt so it can place links in the right sentences.

    ChatGPT package prompt

    Paste your list of post ideas. Aura splits it into batches ChatGPT can finish in one reply, with your affiliate links, the standard and the photo rules built in. Import each ZIP it gives you.

    How each batch works
    1. Copy the batch into ChatGPT. It replies with one ZIP: every post, all SEO fields and a detailed brief for every photo.
    2. Import that ZIP. Aura creates the cover (with the title on it) and the inline photos automatically, one at a time at your speed limit.
    3. Preview, then publish or schedule. Copy the next batch when you are ready.
